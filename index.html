Does this website confirm a collection?

No. It prepares a request locally, without contacting the business or booking a slot. Use the official contact channels to arrange service.

How are commercial volumes calculated?

Estimated kilograms per collection multiplied by collections per week. Four-week volume is four times that weekly estimate, not a calendar-month invoice or price.

What about special fabrics or stains?

Include care-label instructions, fabric, stains and size in your request. Ask the laundry provider to assess suitability before processing.

How should I prepare my items?

Check pockets and remove valuables. Review the provider's published terms and confirm current conditions before service.
